## Foundational release scope

SchoolOS is being developed as a focused, multi-tenant school operations platform. The initial release prioritizes institution setup, admissions, student and guardian records, attendance, timetable, notices, basic fees, exams/results, and syllabus/book lists. Islamic education workflows must be institution-configurable and reviewed by qualified staff; no religious policy or curriculum is hard-coded.

## AI policy

AI features are deferred until permissioned data and core workflows are dependable. AI should draft, extract, or explain; authorized people review and approve official outputs. No autonomous decisions about admissions, discipline, safeguarding, financial aid, marks, or publication.

## Data and safety

Child and guardian data requires verified identity and institution scope, minimal collection, least privilege, auditability, secure retention/export/deletion, and locally reviewed legal processes. Keep demo data synthetic. Do not claim legal compliance based on software alone.
